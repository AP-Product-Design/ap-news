Cyclospora outbreak
5 stories · updated today

DETROIT (AP) — Two people have died in the cyclospora outbreak in Michigan, state health officials said Monday, marking the first U.S. deaths tied to the diarrhea-causing parasite this year.

Both people had underlying health conditions that may have been worsened by the intestinal illness and dehydration, the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services said. Health officials did not release the ages or hometowns of the two people who died.

The outbreak has now been linked to four additional states, federal health officials said last week, as investigators trace contaminated produce through the restaurant supply chain.

Cyclosporiasis is caused by a microscopic parasite typically spread through contaminated food or water. Symptoms include watery diarrhea, loss of appetite, and fatigue, and can last for weeks if untreated.
