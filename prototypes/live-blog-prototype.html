    Live updates: Polls open in four states as voters pick primary winners

    Edited by Nell Clark
    Updated 9:00 AM EDT, August 18, 2026

    What to know:

    • Florida: Voters are choosing a nominee for a special U.S. Senate race, plus deciding an open governor's contest that includes a crowded field on both sides.
    • Alaska: A wide, all-party primary field will get narrowed down to four candidates who advance to a ranked-choice general election in the fall.
    • California: A special congressional election will fill a Bay Area seat left vacant earlier this year; the seat is expected to stay in the same party's hands.
    • Wyoming: Retirements at the top of the ticket have opened up an unusually high number of statewide races for the first time in three decades.
